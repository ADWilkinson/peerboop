## Failure retained and fixed

An earlier best-buy reply chose **Wise at $102.98** even though a cheaper eligible public route existed. That result failed the ranking requirement and was rejected.

The correction makes eligibility filtering and fee-inclusive ranking deterministic. Arithmetic regression coverage is **12 tests plus 29,997 property cases**. The native 1.4.2 replay selected the reconciled **Revolut $100.96** result above. Earlier price captures, including the rejected ranking and superseded Estimated/Indicative presentation, are not the accepted demo evidence.

A source rule alone does not establish native compliance. The tests support the arithmetic and ordering; the actual replay supplies the conversation evidence.

The 1.4.5 live-support test correctly found X Money USD, but incorrectly affirmed Revolut JPY on USDCtoFiat and speculated about buyer wait time from missing recent fills. The actual deployed Revolut selector did not offer JPY. This was rejected. Source 1.4.6 required a positive enabled-selector observation or the final filtered product configuration, rather than a guide, marketing list or raw SDK array. Its holdout still failed: at 14:10:22 the native bot wrongly limited Revolut to USD/EUR/GBP and rejected SGD. The deployed selector and current public payment-method row included SGD. Missing fills establish neither support nor waiting time.

The verified source cause is the product's currency filtering: the upstream SDK catalog is intersected with supported oracle currencies and product policy before the sell selector is built. The existing public payment-method rows use that filtered configuration. Version 1.4.7 reads complete method rows, including collapsed currencies, with a bounded standard-library recipe using existing Grok tools. Its accepted replay is above. No static currency list is copied into the skill. Offline parser tests cover new methods/currencies and malformed or partial rows; an independent review also caught a collapsed-count edge before release.
